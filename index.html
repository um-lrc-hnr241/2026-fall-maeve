Reading Meditations

A collection of reflections, conversations, and images inspired by René Descartes and his Meditations on First Philosophy.

The text & its questions

Begin with a question

What can we know with certainty? In the Meditations, Descartes puts familiar beliefs to the test, asking what remains when he doubts what the senses tell him and even questions the world around him.

Across six meditations, he considers radical doubt, the famous claim “I am, I exist,” the idea of God, and the distinction between mind and body. This portfolio follows our group’s encounters with those arguments: what persuaded us, what puzzled us, and what we are still thinking through.
